Narrative

The Story Behind the Lens & Pixels.

"I combine creativity with technology to build impactful digital projects, bridging the gap between design and security."

I’m Amith S P, a creator passionate about cybersecurity, video editing, and digital design. Since 2019, I've been honing my craft in motion graphics using After Effects, Premiere Pro, and Alight Motion, blending technical precision with artistic vision.

My journey has seen me lead creative strategies as the former CMO at MyWard and current Multimedia Head at Codecave. Today, at Tradex Syndicate, I serve as a Video Editor and Cybersecurity Specialist, where I apply my multidisciplinary skills to secure and enhance digital experiences.

Currently, I am focused on becoming a professional pentester and mastering ethical hacking. To support this journey and others like it, I am developing Hexify, a specialized PWA designed to empower cybersecurity students with accessible tools and resources.
